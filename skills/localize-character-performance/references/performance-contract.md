# Character Performance Contract

## Rights manifest

Record for every likeness, motion, script, translation, and voice:

- source and rights holder;
- consent or license evidence;
- allowed character, lines, languages, territories, media, and commercial use;
- whether transformation, voice cloning, or new speech is allowed;
- retention/deletion date and disclosure requirement.

Unknown authorization blocks rendering. It does not block preparing a plan that identifies the
missing approval.

## Controlled matrix

Keep a row per output:

| variant_id | language | approved text | target seconds | voice source | seed group | status |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

Keep identity anchor, motion reference, camera, blocking, emotion, duration, and provider settings
constant unless that field is the tested variable.

## Review

- Compare face, costume, proportions, gesture beats, gaze, and camera at matching timestamps.
- Review transcript accuracy, pronunciation, meaning, lip-event timing, room tone, and loudness.
- A native/fluent reviewer approves each shipping language.
- Report results per language; do not hide a weak language behind an overall average.
