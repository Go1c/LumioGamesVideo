# Game Marketing Integrity Contract

## Claims sheet

List each mechanic, character, item, environment, offer, price, date, platform, rating, and CTA.
Classify it as:

- **verified** — present and accurate in the current approved build/product;
- **illustrative** — non-gameplay cinematic or mood representation, labeled when needed;
- **pending** — not approved for publication;
- **prohibited** — unavailable, misleading, unlicensed, or platform-disallowed.

Only verified and appropriately labeled illustrative claims may enter a release master.

## Shot labels

Track every timeline segment as captured gameplay, generated cinematic, UI/product render, stock,
or deterministic overlay. Preserve this label in the edit decision log and provenance.

## Deterministic content

Render logos, prices, dates, ratings, legal copy, store badges, platform marks, subtitles, and CTAs
in an editor or engine. Verify localizations and safe areas per platform.

## Release review

Require named approval for gameplay truth, brand, legal/policy, IP/likeness, music/audio,
localization, accessibility/captions, and AI disclosure. A visually polished asset with a false
claim fails review.
